Rasi Phalalu — Rashi-wise Outlook
Each Rashi is shown with its associated birth stars (Nakshatras) — use this to find your own Rashi from your Nakshatra. A star spanning a boundary is listed under both signs with the exact pada (quarter) that belongs to each — check which pada your birth star falls in (your Jathakamu result shows this) to know which Rashi is yours.
Not included: Adaya-Vyaya & Rajapujya-Avamana numbers
Published Panchangams show a numeric Income/Expense ratio (1-14) and Honor/Dishonor ratio (1-7) per rashi. We couldn't find a single, consistently-documented public formula for these specific numbers — different astrologers appear to use their own traditional method. Rather than guess, we've left them out. Your family Panchangam or purohit is the right source for these.
⚠️ Informational only — please consult your purohit
This Panchanga Sravanam is computed from general Vedic mundane-astrology principles (Navanayaka assignments and standard planetary/rashi significations) — the same structure a purohit's reading follows, but without the depth, regional tradition, and personal chart context a real Sravanam includes. Treat it as a helpful overview, and speak with your family purohit for the full, personalized reading.
